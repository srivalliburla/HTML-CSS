Dr. A. P. J.Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of the most respected scientists and presidents of India. He was born onOctober 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams. He played an important role in India's space and missile programs, earning the title “Missile Man of India.”In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. He loved children and encouraged students to dream big and work hard. He received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, in 1997. He passed away on July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture to students. His life teaches us that dedication, education, and hard work can help us achieve great things.
